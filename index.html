THE NARRATIVE

YOU ARE
LUCAS.

A picnic date turns violent when your girlfriend Janaye confronts you — then armed strangers take everything. Bound in an abandoned warehouse, every second counts. Every choice echoes.

Viewers don't just watch — they decide. Sacrifice yourself to protect her? Tell the truth or construct a lie? Bolt for the door or wait for the moment? No two playthroughs are the same.
