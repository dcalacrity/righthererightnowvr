HOW TO
EXPERIENCE IT

Never used VR before? No problem. Here's exactly how to get into the film — it takes less than five minutes and costs nothing.

01

GET A META QUEST

Any Meta Quest 2, 3, or 3S headset works. Don't own one? Borrow from a friend, check your local library's tech lending program, or visit a gaming cafe. They're more common than you'd think.

02

FIND THE FILM

We're now live on the Meta Horizon Store — search "Right Here Right Now" directly in your headset or browser. SideQuest also works. It's completely free, no subscription required.

03

MAKE YOUR CHOICES

Put on the headset. You're Lucas. When the film presents a decision, press the trigger. Every choice changes what happens next. Play it more than once — different paths lead to very different endings.

HOW IT'S MADE

THE PRODUCTION
PIPELINE

Four phases from multipathway screenplay to shipped APK. Every bottleneck solved. The process scales.

01
MULTIPATHWAY SCRIPT

Multipathway screenplay by Tommy Bowers — authored in Alacrity Hub's Script Studio with branching dialogue, parallel tracks, and consequence logic in one unified environment.

02
NODE MAP & DESIGN

Every scene mapped as a node. Emotional tension metrics and state variables set before a single frame is shot.

03
11K LIVE CAPTURE

Insta360 Titan on set — every branch filmed as a practical scene. The viewer is always centered in a 360° world.

04
UNITY INTEGRATION

Hub exports a JSON bundle parsed directly by Unity. Branch logic and spatial audio deployed to Meta Quest with no manual rebuild.

PRODUCTION
BUILT FOR 360°

Sets designed from the start for spatial capture — blocking, sight lines, and lighting optimized for VR presence rather than a flat frame.

DEVELOPMENT BASE
IFML MEDIA LAB · UNCW

The Intersectional Feminist Media Lab at UNCW — where Alacrity Hub was developed alongside the screenplay, shot list, and node architecture.

DELIVERY
NODE TO HEADSET

The multipathway screenplay lives in Alacrity Hub — branching dialogue, parallel tracks, and consequence logic authored by Tommy Bowers, then mapped directly to the node graph that ships to headset.
