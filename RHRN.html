The Project

What is
RHRN?

RHRN is a groundbreaking interactive film that obliterates the wall between audience and author. Produced by UNCW film students, it's a fully-staged, choose-your-own-adventure narrative — live-acted in the black box theater, filmed with a full crew, and experienced through immersive VR.


Viewers don't just watch — they decide. Every choice forks the story. Every path is different. No two screenings are the same.

How We Made It

The Process

01

Script Development

Crafting a branching screenplay where every decision point felt organic and consequential — multiple threads woven into one world.

02

Live Production

Staged and filmed in UNCW's black box theater. Every scene performed multiple times to capture all outcomes of the branching story.

03

Interactive Edit

Editing non-linearly — building an interactive architecture that enables seamless narrative switching based on viewer choices.

04

VR Delivery

Integrated into a Meta Quest VR environment where viewers experience the film and interact with the branching story using motion controllers.

The Experience

Cinema where you hold the remote.

Using a Meta Quest VR headset, audiences step inside the film. The story unfolds all around them — and when the moment of decision arrives, they reach out and choose. What happens next is entirely theirs.
